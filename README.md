# BioWorth

**BioWorth** is a **user-friendly, web-based economic assessment tool** developed to support **green biorefinery evaluation** in a simple, practical, and accessible way.

Designed especially for **non-expert users**, BioWorth helps researchers, students, project partners, and decision-makers perform preliminary economic assessments **without requiring advanced expertise in techno-economic analysis**. Through an intuitive interface, users can enter key process and cost parameters and quickly obtain meaningful economic insights.

## Why BioWorth?

Economic assessment is often challenging for users who do not have a strong background in process economics, cost engineering, or techno-economic analysis. BioWorth addresses this gap by offering a tool that is:

- **Easy to use**
- **Designed for non-experts**
- **Fast and practical for preliminary assessments**
- **Supportive for decision-making**
- **Visually clear and informative**

Rather than forcing users to work with complex spreadsheets or specialized software, BioWorth provides a more approachable environment for evaluating economic performance.

## What BioWorth Can Do

BioWorth is more than a simple calculator. It is a **decision-support tool** that helps users better understand the economic implications of their process choices.

### Key capabilities include:

- **CAPEX estimation**  
  Evaluate capital expenditure based on process and equipment-related inputs.

- **OPEX estimation**  
  Estimate operating expenditure using user-defined economic and operational parameters.

- **Economic indicator calculation**  
  Generate key outputs that support preliminary economic analysis.

- **User guidance and practical advice**  
  Help users understand what inputs are needed and how results can support decision-making.

- **Clear presentation of results**  
  Depict and organize outputs in a structured and understandable way for easier interpretation.

- **Accessible design for non-specialists**  
  Built with usability in mind so that non-expert users can work confidently with the tool.

## Who Is It For?

BioWorth is intended for:

- Researchers
- Students
- Project partners
- Engineers in early-stage assessment
- Non-expert users interested in economic screening
- Stakeholders involved in green biorefinery and bio-based process development

## Project Vision

BioWorth has been developed to make **economic assessment more understandable, accessible, and actionable**.  
Its goal is to reduce complexity, support early-stage evaluation, and help users move from raw input data to useful economic insight with confidence.

By combining **practical functionality**, **user-friendly design**, and **decision-support value**, BioWorth aims to bridge the gap between advanced economic analysis and everyday project needs.

## Project Status

BioWorth is currently a **research and development prototype**.  
It is intended for **preliminary assessment, educational purposes, and research-oriented applications**.

While the tool provides useful economic insights, results should be interpreted carefully and may require expert review before being used for detailed investment or commercial decisions.
